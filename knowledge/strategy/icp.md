## Primary ICPs

### ICP-1: Classical / Opera Singer

**Who:** Conservatory students, pre-professional singers, 18–45. Self-directed learners between lessons.

**Pain:**
- Can't tell if vibrato is improving between lessons
- Coaches give subjective feedback ("more spin" / "less wobble") — no objective anchor
- Want to show up to lessons with data, not just feelings

**Jobs to be done:**
- Measure vibrato width and rate objectively
- Track pitch accuracy over repeated practice runs
- Generate evidence of improvement for audition prep

**WTP:** $15–25/mo or $10–20/analysis

**Where to find:** r/classicalmusic, NATS forums (National Association of Teachers of Singing), conservatory Discord servers, TalkClassical, VocalProcess forums

**Acquisition hook:** "Here's what a vibrato problem actually looks like in signal data"

---

### ICP-2: Musical Theatre Singer

**Who:** 16–35, audition-driven. Pre-audition prep is high-stakes. "Am I ready?" is a live question with money/opportunity on the line.

**Pain:**
- Need data, not feelings, before auditions
- Self-assessment is unreliable; coaches are expensive and scheduling-constrained
- Can hear "something's off" but can't locate the problem

**Jobs to be done:**
- Verify pitch accuracy in specific phrases
- Check vibrato consistency across a song
- Build confidence through objective measurement

**WTP:** $10–20/mo

**Where to find:** r/musicaltheatre, Broadway/MT Discord servers, BroadwayWorld forums, acting conservatory communities

---

### ICP-3: Indie / Singer-Songwriter

**Who:** 20–40, home studio, self-produces. Records themselves, mixes themselves. Has taste, lacks measurement tools.

**Pain:**
- Can hear "something's off" but can't diagnose it
- Auto-Tune and correction tools mask problems without identifying them
- No access to professional ears; coaches are expensive or don't understand their genre

**Jobs to be done:**
- Identify specific pitch or dynamics issues in a track
- Decide whether a take is good before committing to it in a mix
- Understand their artistic signature (vibrato, dynamics range, spectral character)

**WTP:** $10–20/analysis (pay-as-you-go)

**Where to find:** r/WeAreTheMusicMakers (1.7M), r/songwriting, home studio Discord servers, GearSlutz/Gearspace forums

---

### ICP-4: Vocal Coach (B2B)

**Who:** Independent vocal teacher, 5–30 students. Tech-forward, uses online tools for scheduling and video lessons.

**Pain:**
- Student progress is invisible between lessons
- Subjective feedback ("that phrase had more spin") is hard for students to internalize
- No shared data layer between coach and student

**Jobs to be done:**
- Generate chart data during or after a lesson
- Share annotated charts with students as homework
- Track student progress over time with objective comparisons

**WTP:** $40–60/mo (Studio tier); would pay for bulk student access

**Where to find:** Vocal coaches on Instagram, NATS member directory, One Voice (vocal education community), Big Brain Singer community

**Acquisition hook:** Affiliate model — free Pro account → refer students → students subscribe

---

### ICP-5: Music Student (University)

**Who:** University vocal student, 18–24. In a structured program with recitals, jury recordings, applied lessons.

**Pain:**
- Need to show progress to professor/advisor
- Jury recordings are high-stakes — want objective self-assessment beforehand
- Limited budget; institutional pricing matters

**WTP:** Low individually — better addressed via university licensing

**Channel:** Institutional outreach to music department faculty

## Secondary ICPs (future)

- **Podcast hosts** — voice quality consistency, dynamics analysis
- **Instrument educators** — pitch accuracy for instrumentalists (not vocals-specific)
- **Music production teachers** — dynamics and frequency content analysis for production courses

---

## ICP Priority Order for Launch

1. **Vocal coaches** — fastest to convert, high WTP, multiplier effect (they bring students)
2. **Classical/opera singers** — most analytically sophisticated, least likely to distrust data
3. **Musical theatre singers** — large community, high urgency around auditions
4. **Singer-songwriters** — large audience, lower WTP per head but very large TAM
