## Gap Summary

Grain is the only product with all four:
1. **Consumer-accessible pYIN pitch analysis** — Tony/Praat do it, but aren't consumer-ready
2. **Vibrato width over time** — only VoceVista Pro ($399 desktop) does this well
3. **"No AI judgment" framing** — contrarian in a market full of opaque AI scores
4. **Charts + AI interpretation + human review in one workflow** — no competitor has all three

---

## Positioning Statement

<!-- Stub -->

Grain is the first web-based audio analysis tool that gives musicians objective signal-level data — not AI judgments, not scores — with an optional expert human review layer. It sits at the intersection of scientific rigor (pYIN, Demucs) and consumer accessibility (web upload, Flutter UI, free tier).

## Strategic Implications

1. **Desktop incumbents are undefended on web.** VoceVista Pro and Sing & See have no web offering. Any web-first product wins the accessibility comparison.
2. **The "no judgment" angle is a genuine differentiator.** Singing Coach AI and Rate My Voice lean into opaque AI scores. Grain's "measurements not judgments" framing is unusual and resonates with serious musicians who distrust AI opinions.
3. **Human reviews anchor Grain in the premium market.** Gene Coye or Jake Chapman reviewing your audio is more valuable than any AI feature. This is the long-term moat — no algorithm replicates it.
4. **Free charts as a growth lever.** No competitor offers free chart download. Every shared PNG is a free impression with grain.audio on it.
