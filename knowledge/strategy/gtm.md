## GTM Philosophy

Grain's GTM is community-first, data-led. The product's unique angle — "measurements not judgments" — is best demonstrated, not explained. Sample charts in relevant communities drive organic discovery. Vocal coaches as affiliates create a multiplier effect.

## Launch Sequence

### Pre-Launch (Before Beta)

1. **Waitlist landing page at grain.audio**
   - Above the fold: product hook + sample chart PNG
   - Email capture (no credit card)
   - "See your voice" CTA
   - Risograph visual identity from day one

2. **Community seeding (2–3 posts)**
   - r/singing: "What does a vibrato problem actually look like?" — show before/after chart
   - r/classicalmusic: pitch track analysis angle
   - Vocal Academy Discord: DM to mods, ask for feedback channel post

3. **Founding reviewer outreach**
   - Jake, Gene, Sophie: personal DM from Alice
   - Offer: free lifetime Pro account + "founding reviewer" badge + set your own prices
   - Ask for a sample review to use in launch materials

### Beta (Invite Waitlist)

1. Invite first 50–100 from waitlist
2. 5 free analyses per user (no credit card)
3. Recruit 5 vocal coaches for testimonials — offer lifetime Pro account in exchange
4. Collect feedback on: chart quality, annotation UX, what's missing
5. Iterate on the most common issues before public launch

### Public Launch

1. Product Hunt post (coordinate timing with community posts)
2. Show HN post (technical audience)
3. r/singing + r/classicalmusic launch posts with sample charts
4. Vocal coach outreach: 10 cold emails to coaches found via NATS directory or Instagram
5. Music blog pitches: 5 targeted blogs covering vocal technique or music tech

## Product Hunt Hook

> "Upload your singing. Get a real pitch track, vibrato chart, and dynamics plot. No AI grade. Just data."

Supporting copy:
- "VoceVista Pro costs $399 and runs only on Windows. We built Grain to be web-native and free to try."
- "The AI doesn't tell you if you're good or bad. It shows you your frequency content, pitch confidence, and vibrato width over time. You decide what it means."
- "Human experts (real session musicians and coaches) can review your analysis if you want a second opinion."

---

## Messaging Framework

### Primary hook
"You already know your voice. Now see it."

### Elevator pitch
Grain is audio analysis for musicians. Upload any recording — vocals, instrument, full mix — and get a signal-level readout: spectrogram, pitch track, vibrato width, dynamics. No AI grade. Just data. Expert human review available.

### For vocal coaches
"Give your students a shared data language. They practice, they upload, they bring you charts. You spend your lesson time on interpretation, not diagnosis."

### For serious singers
"VoceVista Pro is $399 and desktop-only. Sing & See hasn't shipped a feature since 2016. We built the web-native alternative — same signal rigor, but accessible."

### For singer-songwriters
"You can hear something's off. Now you can see it. Upload the take, find the phrase, fix the problem."

## Notes

- **Do not gate waitlist behind credit card.** Friction kills top-of-funnel. Email only.
- **Free chart PNG = viral loop.** Every shared chart should be watermarked with grain.audio.
- **Vocal coaches are the force multiplier.** One coach with 20 students = 20 potential users. Prioritize coach acquisition.
- **NATS is the highest-leverage channel in the classical/opera market.** If one NATS board member endorses Grain, it propagates through the network.
