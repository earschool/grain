# Change 009: AI Explain Mode

**Status:** Draft stub
**Priority:** P2
**Phase:** 3

## What

LLM produces a plain-language written explanation of what the charts show. For users who prefer to read rather than visually explore.

## Credit Tiers

| Level | Model | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| Summary | Haiku | 5 |
| Deep | Sonnet | 20 |
| Expert | Opus | 50 |

## Key Design Rule (Locked)

Observational, not judgmental. "Your pitch track shows..." not "Your pitch is bad." Assumptions stated explicitly.

## Acceptance Criteria (stub)

- [ ] Three credit tiers available
- [ ] Output is plain-language text, not chart overlays
- [ ] Framing is observational throughout
- [ ] Assumptions about genre/style declared
