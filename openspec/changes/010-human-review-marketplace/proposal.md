# Change 010: Human Review Marketplace

**Status:** Draft stub
**Priority:** P3
**Phase:** 4

## What

Async critique from vetted professional musicians and vocal coaches. Reviewer sees charts alongside audio. Reviewer sets own price. Platform takes 20–25% commission.

## Why

Human expert feedback is rare and valuable. $50 from Gene Coye > 10 AI analyses. Grain provides the signal-level anchor that no other marketplace provides.
